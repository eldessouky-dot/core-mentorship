The mentorship

Sharp where it counts. Relaxed about the rest.

Core is a one-on-one mentorship for people who want to build a career that looks and sounds like them. It's built on respect for the craft and a refusal to follow the script. Smart casual, in other words.

We don't chase quick fixes. We look at how you think, decide and show up, find what's quietly draining you, and build the habits and standards that let your best work happen on purpose, not by accident.

The approach

A method, not a template.

Core follows a deliberate sequence, from getting honest about where you stand to performing when it counts. Each step builds on the last.

The specifics unfold in our sessions, shaped around you, your work and the turning point you're at.

Your mentor

Islam ElDessouky

Islam has spent nearly two decades leading creative and marketing work for one of the world's best-known brands, building big ideas that travel and then getting out of the way so local teams can make them their own.

Having lived in eight countries, he learned early that the same story lands differently depending on where you hear it. The same is true of careers. He started Core to help people stop borrowing someone else's script and write their own.

He credits his mother as his school of life. Off the clock, it's basketball, sneakers, hip-hop and a BBQ pit he has strong opinions about.
